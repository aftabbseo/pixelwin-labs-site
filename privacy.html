Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

In short

  • Our apps have no accounts and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your own device.
  • Our apps may show ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID.
  • Our apps may send crash reports and anonymous usage statistics through Google Firebase, so that we can fix problems and improve them.
  • Purchases inside our apps are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the mobile apps and games published by PixelWin Labs, India ("we", "us", "our"), including Pocket Puzzles: Offline Games, and to this website. If you have a question about this policy or about your data, write to pixelwinlabs@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Our apps save information such as your progress, scores, in-app rewards and settings on your device only, so that you can continue where you left off. A nickname you choose to type in is kept on your device and is not sent to us. We do not run servers that receive this information.

3. Advertising

Our apps may show ads provided by Google AdMob: optional videos you can choose to watch for an in-app reward, and full-screen ads that may appear at a natural break, for example after you win a level. To show and measure ads, Google collects and processes:

  • the advertising ID of your device;
  • your IP address, which indicates your approximate location (country or region);
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • how you interact with ads (shown, watched, tapped).

Depending on your choices and where you live, Google may use this information to show personalised ads. Where the law requires it, our apps ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases

Some of our apps offer optional in-app purchases, such as "Remove Ads". They are made through Google Play's billing system: Google collects and processes your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy, and we never receive your card number, bank details, name or address. The app receives from Google Play only a confirmation of the purchase (the product, an order and purchase reference, and whether the payment is complete, pending or refunded), and checks with Google Play from time to time whether the purchase is still owned, so that it can be restored on a new device or withdrawn after a refund. Google Play may send us order reports (order reference, product, price, country and status) for our accounts and tax records.

5. Network requests made by our apps

Besides the services named in this policy, an app may ask a Google server for the current date, so that daily rewards follow the real calendar. Such a request contains no information about you; like any internet connection it shows your IP address to that server.

6. Crash reports and usage statistics

  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when an app crashes or hits an error: what went wrong, the app version, the device model and operating system version, and an installation identifier. We use it only to find and fix problems.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase records usage events, for example that the app was opened, a level was finished or an in-app item was unlocked, together with general device information, the country derived from your IP address, and an app-instance identifier. We use it to understand how our apps are used and to improve them.

These reports do not contain your name or contact details. Google processes this data on our behalf; see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Children

Our apps are meant for adults: they are for people aged 18 or older and are not directed at children (anyone under 18). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used one of our apps and personal information about them was collected, contact us and we will delete what we can and ask our service providers to do the same.

9. Backup

If backup is turned on in your device's Google settings, your saved progress and settings may be included in your device's own backup and restored when you reinstall an app or move to a new device. That backup is kept in your Google account, not by us.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Advertising ID: in your device's settings you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation.
  • Consent: where a consent form was shown, you can change your answer from the same form in the app.
  • Purchases: are managed and refunded through Google Play, under Google Play's refund policies.
  • Data on your device: clearing an app's storage or uninstalling it removes your progress and settings from the device.
  • Requests: to ask about, or request deletion of, crash and usage data linked to your installation, write to us at the address above.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local data protection law, such as access, correction, deletion or objection. We will respond to such requests within a reasonable time.

11. How long data is kept

Crash reports are kept for about 90 days. Usage event data tied to an installation identifier is kept for up to 14 months and after that only in aggregated form. Order reports are kept for as long as tax law requires. Data held by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the update notes of the affected apps.
